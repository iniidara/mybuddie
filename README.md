# MyBuddie

A social learning platform designed to help students connect, share assignments, and collaborate on academic subjects.

## Overview

MyBuddie is a web-based application that enables students to discover trending academic topics, share resources, and find answers to their questions across various subjects including Biology, Mathematics, History, Chemistry, and English.

## Features

- **User Authentication**: Secure login and signup functionality
- **Timeline Feed**: View trending subjects and recent academic content
- **Trending Topics**: Discover popular subjects with answer counts
  - Biology
  - Mathematics
  - History
  - Chemistry
  - English
- **User Profiles**: Create and manage your student profile
- **Search Functionality**: Find assignments and subjects easily
- **Responsive Design**: Works seamlessly on desktop and mobile devices

## Getting Started

1. **Open the application**: Start by opening `login.html` in your web browser
2. **Create an account**: Click "Sign up" to create a new account
3. **Log in**: Use your credentials to access the timeline
4. **Explore**: View trending subjects and connect with other students

## Usage

### Signup
- Enter your first and last name
- Select your gender
- Provide your email and password
- Confirm your password

### Login
- Enter your email
- Enter your password
- Click "Log in" to access the timeline

### Timeline
- Search for assignments and subjects using the search box
- Browse trending topics sorted by popularity
- Click on a trending item to view more details and answers
