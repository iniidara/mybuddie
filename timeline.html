Question: Solve the integral of x²sin(x)dx

Use integration by parts twice. Let u = x², dv = sin(x)dx. First iteration gives you the reduction formula. The final answer is -x²cos(x) + 2xsin(x) + 2cos(x) + C. I've verified this by differentiation!

Essay: The Impact of Mitochondrial Function on Cellular Energy Production

Mitochondria produce ATP through oxidative phosphorylation in the cristae. The inner membrane has a large surface area which increases efficiency. Cells with high energy demands (muscle, nerve) contain more mitochondria. This is why athletes have enhanced mitochondrial density!

Literary Analysis: Symbolism in The Great Gatsby

The green light represents Gatsby's dreams and the unreachable American Dream. The eyes of Dr. T.J. Eckleburg symbolize God's judgment over the morality of the characters. Fitzgerald's use of color symbolism throughout reinforces themes of wealth, corruption, and desire.
